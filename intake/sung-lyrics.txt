Abram was ninety-nine years old
when the Lord appeared to him:
“I am your God. Be well-pleasing before me,
and be blameless.”
“I will make my covenant between us,
and multiply you beyond measure.”
Abram fell facedown,
and God spoke to him:
“As for me, this is my covenant with you:
you will father a multitude of nations.”
“You will no longer be called Abram.
Your name will be Abraam,
for I have made you father of many nations.”
“I will make you exceedingly fruitful.
Nations and kings will come from you.”
“I will keep my covenant with you
and your descendants through every generation,
an everlasting covenant: I will be your God
and the God of your descendants.”
“I will give you and your descendants
all the land of Chanaan where you live as a stranger,
an everlasting possession; I will be their God.”
God said to Abraam,
“You and your descendants through every generation
must fully keep my covenant.”
“This is the covenant you must fully keep
between me and you,
and your descendants through their generations:
every male among you must be circumcised.”
“You shall be circumcised in the flesh
of your foreskin. This will be the sign
of the covenant between us.”
“Every boy, eight days old, must be circumcised
throughout your generations—those born in your house,
and those bought with money from strangers,
who are not your descendants.”
“Those born in your house and those bought
with money must be circumcised.
My covenant will be marked on your flesh,
an everlasting covenant.”
“The male not circumcised in the flesh
of his foreskin on the eighth day—
that soul shall be utterly destroyed from its family,
for he has broken my covenant.”
God said to Abraam,
“Your wife Sara shall no longer be called Sara;
her name will be Sarrha.”
“I will bless her and give you a son by her.
I will bless him; he will become nations,
and kings of nations will come from him.”
Abraam fell facedown and laughed,
saying in his heart, “Can a child be born
to a man of a hundred? Can Sarrha,
at ninety years old, bear a child?”
Abraam said to God,
“May Ismael live before you!”
God said to Abraam, “Yes. Sarrha your wife
will bear you a son. You shall name him Isaac.
I will establish my covenant with him,
an everlasting covenant,
to be God to him and his seed after him.”
“As for Ismael, I have heard you.
I have blessed him; I will make him fruitful
and multiply him greatly. He will father
twelve nations, and I will make him a great nation.”
“But I will establish my covenant with Isaac,
whom Sarrha will bear to you at this season
next year.”
When God had finished speaking with Abraam,
God went up from him.
Abraam took Ismael his son,
all born in his house and all bought with money,
every male in his household, and circumcised them
that very day, as God had told him.
Abraam was ninety-nine years old
when he was circumcised.
Ismael his son was thirteen years old
when he was circumcised.
That very day Abraam was circumcised,
and Ismael his son,
and every man in his household,
born in the house or bought with money
from foreigners.
from foreigners.
