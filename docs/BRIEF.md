# Genesis 17, A New Name: director brief

**Mode:** code only, 1920×1080, 60 fps, 16 samples.

- **One world:** the hill of Mambre near Hebron, with its great oak, in spring. One day, from stars to stars.
- **Objects act:** the staff is Abram, then Abraam. The red cloth is Sara, then Sarrha; a gold hem comes at her
  name. The short staff with the indigo cloth is Ismael. The empty cradle is Isaac. Lamps, staffs and rounds of
  clay are the men of the household.
- **The covenant** is one cord of two strands, flax and light. When the flax is broken, the light stays.
- **The name** is a seal: the old mark is washed from the clay, and a new one is rolled into it.
- **The sign** is never shown. A seal is pressed in clay, a curtain closes, a lamp is lit. No cut, no blood.
- **God** is light, from above, and nothing else. Signal colour 255, 218, 150: his light and his words.
- **Type:** EB Garamond; Inter Tight for the annotation. Narrator roman, Abraam italic, the Lord small capitals.
- **Palette:** stars, predawn rose, sunrise gold, white noon, warm afternoon, golden hour, dusk, stars.
- **Light on Ismael too:** he is heard and blessed (verse 20); the covenant is named through Isaac (verse 21).
